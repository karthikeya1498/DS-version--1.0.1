# OPTIMA-X Unified Data Ecosystem

OPTIMA-X now uses a focused data ecosystem rather than an arbitrary collection of files. Each source has one primary responsibility and is connected through time, zone, and graph identifiers in processed products.

| Source | Local artifact | Role | Size / scale |
|---|---|---|---:|
| OpenStreetMap Overpass | `data/raw/osm/manhattan_roads.json` | Road topology and geometry | 40 MB; 176,597 graph nodes and 407,571 directed edges |
| NYC TLC Yellow Taxi | `data/raw/traffic/yellow_tripdata_2024-01.parquet` | Mobility-derived traffic and zone demand proxy | 2,964,624 source trips; 77,524 hourly-zone aggregates |
| NOAA GHCN Daily | `data/raw/weather/USW00094728.csv.gz` | Historical weather covariates | 1.8 MB; 57,578 clean daily records |
| UCI Bike Sharing | `data/raw/demand/bike_sharing.zip` | Compact real demand forecasting benchmark | 276 KB archive |
| UCI Logistics Orders | `data/raw/orders/daily_demand.zip` | Independent logistics-demand validation | 8 KB archive |
| US holiday rules | `data/raw/events/us_holidays_2024.csv` | Holiday/event indicators | Generated from explicit US holiday rules |
| Synthetic fleet | Generated by simulator | Capacity, availability, and shift controls | Reproducible from a seed |

## Pipeline

`download_phase1_datasets.py` downloads raw sources and writes SHA-256 manifests. `prepare_unified_dataset.py` converts the NYC trip Parquet and NOAA fixed-width CSV into `data/processed/unified/traffic_hourly.csv`, `demand_hourly_from_tlc.csv`, and `weather_daily.csv`. `build_osm_graph.py` converts the Overpass JSON geometry response into the OPTIMA-X `RoadGraph` abstraction and writes a graph summary.

The large raw files are intentionally not committed to Git. They are reproducible from the URLs in `data/raw/dataset_manifest.json`, while the scripts, manifests, hashes, schemas, and processed outputs define the exact acquisition and transformation contract.

## Provenance and limitations

The OSM extract is bounded to Manhattan and requires OpenStreetMap attribution. NYC TLC trip records are a mobility proxy, not delivery orders; demand and traffic are derived by pickup zone and hourly aggregation. Weather is from the NOAA Central Park station archive. The UCI datasets are retained for forecasting benchmarks and independent validation, while fleet data remains controlled synthetic simulation data.
